\section{Provisional console scale evidence}\label{sec:scale-console}
The scale queue reported all three alternatives complete with zero worker exit
codes on 23 October 2026 at 10:57 Tehran time. Traffic-quota exhaustion prevents
byte-preserving recovery of the final server files. Table~\ref{tab:scale-console}
therefore separates console-transcribed timings from fresh local numerical
checks of the SHA256-pinned inputs uploaded for this queue. It is a supervisor
review result, not an independently replayed extension of the frozen proof
capture. No newly completed main-campaign cases are silently pooled into the
earlier tables.

The matched scale workload uses MNIST, $N=1024$, $k=3$, seed 11, rank 63 for
both compressed representations, $T=16384$, batch 4, one timed repetition and
no warmup. All alternatives use the same catalog and six center sets, including
four actual Lloyd updates, a null and a reverse diagnostic. This extends a
frozen workload to a larger catalog; it is not an independent model fit.

The transcribed Full cost is 15,072.3454\,s, compared with 7,176.6661\,s for
head and 7,182.2011\,s for PCA: a 2.10-fold Full/head ratio and 52.39\% time
reduction on this boundary. Full workflow wall time is separately
15,144.1578\,s and is not used in that ratio. Console summaries report all four
actual update signs conclusive for both compressed alternatives. The local
integer reference checks agree and find no false certified assignments;
mean absolute cost errors are 0.00919\% for head and 0.01272\% for PCA.
A conclusive cost-change sign does not imply exact aggregate costs or fully
certified assignments. Head and PCA are practically tied in measured time,
and their small agreement difference on one fit does not establish general
representation superiority. Original summaries, decisions, hashes and public
proofs must be recovered and independently replayed before these provisional
results become final archival evidence.
