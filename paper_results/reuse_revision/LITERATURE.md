Checked 19 October 2026 against the primary sources linked below.

- [Feldman, Schmidt and Sohler, SODA 2013](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/1.9781611973105.103), pp. 1434–1453. The [extended version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.04518) gives the matrix Pythagorean identity in Claim 3 (printed p. 5), and explicitly decomposes distance to centers in a subspace into projected distance plus residual cost in Eq. (9) (printed p. 22). The [journal version](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/18M1209854) is SIAM Journal on Computing 49(3), 601–657 (2020). This is direct prior art for projection with an additive residual offset. The manuscript attributes the geometry and claims the authenticated per-record representation, integer implementation and reuse measurements.
- [Cohen, Elder, Musco, Musco and Persu, STOC 2015](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1410.6801), especially Definitions 1–2 in §3 (printed p. 7): projection-cost-preserving sketches allow an additive constant independent of the queried projection. This broader approximation framework is related, while the fixed-center Pythagorean specialization here is elementary.
- [Patel, Patel and Jinwala, ICDcit 2013](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-642-36071-8_33), pp. 420–431. The publisher abstract describes horizontally partitioned private k-means using secret sharing and code-based zero-knowledge identification against malicious participants. Only the accessible abstract and bibliographic metadata were inspected; no claims about detailed circuit performance are made.
- [Repetto et al., PAC to the Future (2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.11954v1), §§4.3.1, 5.3.1, 6.2: PAC-private k-means mechanisms and RISC-Zero verification. The paper has a different privacy/output goal; it rules out claiming that proving k-means in zero knowledge is a new category.
- [Qiu et al., V3DB (2026 preprint)](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.03065v1): verification of published approximate vector-search semantics over committed snapshots. This is nearby committed-vector query work; no cross-system timing superiority is inferred.
- [Chiesa, Tromer and Virza, Cluster Computing in Zero Knowledge (2015)](https://eprint.iacr.org/2015/377) concerns distributed computer clusters. Its title is a search false positive for statistical clustering and is not used as a direct k-means baseline.

The existing related-work coverage of ezDPS and Artemis is retained. The new bibliography entries also identify the embedding sources, including the [all-MiniLM-L6-v2 model card](https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2).

This is a focused prior-art check, not an exhaustive novelty certification. No “first ZK clustering system,” new coreset identity, or universal learned-map fidelity claim is made.
