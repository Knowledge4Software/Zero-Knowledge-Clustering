\subsection{Larger frozen-profile native confirmation}
The extended confirmation fixes the prediction rank at 63, the certificate
rank at 79, 14-bit basis precision and the 16-row width-ordered refinement
cap before evaluating four new seeds (173, 191, 211 and 223) on each dataset.
There are eight dataset/seed splits, with paired reference ages and bases;
the resulting 56 representation fits are not 56 independent datasets.
Each source sample contains 50,000 real records, with 3,000 training rows
and disjoint held-out rows. Input scaling and both bases use training data
only. The $k=5$ study uses all four seeds. The $k=3,10,20$ sweep uses seed
173, and every distinct declared center proposal is retained.

Table~\ref{tab:optimized-confirmation} separates sign resolution, regression
sensitivity, predictor agreement and assignment certificates on 1,024 rows.
At $k=5$, the centroid instantiation resolves all 60 distinct candidate
decisions, including five natural regressions, without exact-row refinement;
PCA reaches the same decision count using 14 exact rows. Mean centroid
agreement is 99.942\% on MNIST and 99.948\% on Fashion-MNIST. On the primary
16,384-row MNIST scale catalog it resolves 4/4 updates, with 99.786\% mean
and 99.762\% worst agreement.
The stress sweep exposes a remaining boundary: Fashion-MNIST at $k=10$
resolves 6/8 updates and 2/3 regressions with either basis, so the two
remaining updates require exact fallback. One centroid request at $k=20$
on MNIST has 98.926\% agreement. Thus neither complete decision coverage
nor the working agreement target holds uniformly across the sweep.
Inconclusive outcomes and requests below the working 99\% agreement target
remain in the evidence. That target is an experimental requirement, not a
universal acceptance threshold. Exact-row corrections tighten the aggregate
change interval; they do not change the frozen predictor's labels.
The center-count sweep and real-row scale series through 16,384 records are
reported in Tables~\ref{tab:optimized-centers} and~\ref{tab:optimized-scale}.
These are native exact-integer checks. They establish neither large-catalog
proof speed nor absence of fallback costs in a proof deployment.

The new completed proof controls in Table~\ref{tab:optimized-proofs}
include a 1,024-record Fashion-MNIST catalog ($k=5$, seed 173, reference
age 8). All three distinct natural worsening proposals involve assignment
switching (3, 9 and 10 records) and are certified by the centroid
instantiation without exact-row correction or Full fallback. Observed
assignment agreement is 100\% for these three requests; individually
certified assignment coverage is 99.707--99.902\%. The measured component
total is 42.534 minutes, versus 101.575 minutes for reusable exact Full,
a 2.388-fold ratio. Equally refined PCA certifies the same three changes
in 44.156 minutes, using three exact-row corrections (2.300-fold versus
Full). The authenticated moments-only control resolves none of these
worsening updates before fallback and costs 134.413 minutes including
fresh Full generation. These are one-repetition observations on one fit,
not evidence of uniform representation superiority or deployment economics.
The two 128-record MNIST controls each have three completed timing
repetitions; repeated requests do not constitute independent proposals.
