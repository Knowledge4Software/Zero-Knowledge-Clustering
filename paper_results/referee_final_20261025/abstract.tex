\begin{abstract}
We study release-time audits of candidate prototype banks on a fixed,
owner-committed feature catalog. Several candidates are compared with the
same deployed reference before a replacement is installed. Authenticated
group moments give the exact cost change under the old assignments; compact
residual bounds cover reassignment. Authenticated row corrections tighten
ambiguous intervals before exact fallback. The resulting integer-safe
certificate supports strict decisions about quantized clustering cost with
either PCA or a centroid-informed representation. On a 1,024-record
Fashion-MNIST workload it certifies all three naturally worsening updates
with switching in 42.534 minutes, versus 101.575 minutes for reusable exact
Groth16 evaluation, a 2.388-fold measured cost ratio. Sensor features and
pretrained neural text embeddings extend the application checks to
new-training-minibatch proposals, including inconclusive outcomes. The
certificate concerns clustering-cost change, and its public aggregates
permit group-statistics inference; it does not provide adaptive record
non-reconstruction.
\end{abstract}
