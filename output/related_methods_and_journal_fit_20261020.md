# Related methods and Neurocomputing fit - 20 October 2026

This is a bounded literature check, not a proof of novelty or an exhaustive systematic review. Existing papers cover the geometric identity, projection-based ZK checks, private/verifiable clustering, PCA in zkML, and audits over committed vector snapshots. The exact combination implemented here was not identified in the checked papers: original-row-bound head/energy preprocessing, reuse across changing public center sets, rounded-basis assignment-count and original-space quantized-cost certificates, plus a separate accuracy acceptance policy.

## Journal fit: primary evidence

- [Elsevier's official Neurocomputing description](https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/neurocomputing/0925-2312) covers learning systems, machine learning, pattern recognition, theory, practice and applications. The main ScienceDirect aims-and-scope URL was unavailable through this browsing route; the publisher description was accessible.
- [Practical multi-party private collaborative k-means clustering](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0925231221014284), Neurocomputing 467 (2022), 256-265, DOI 10.1016/j.neucom.2021.09.050: secure multiparty computation plus differential privacy for private clustering. This is topical precedent, not the same proof system.
- [Privacy-preserving nearest prototype classifier](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0925231226000706), Neurocomputing 671 (2026), 132673: TFHE/LVQ-1, dimensionality and integer-encoding considerations. This is particularly close application precedent; encryption and zero-knowledge auditing provide different guarantees.
- [Lightweight verifiable federated learning based on matrix theory](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0925231225012512), Neurocomputing 647 (2025), 130579: privacy and verification systems within ML are represented in the journal. It does not establish acceptance of our contribution.

Assessment: Neurocomputing is a plausible target by scope and published precedents. Submission readiness depends on the completed fair-baseline evaluation, practical certified utility, clear comparison with the papers above, and a precise systems contribution. Neither standard geometry/Groth16 nor a claimed universal learned-over-PCA improvement should supply the novelty claim. Existing weak strict-policy coverage remains a material scientific limitation. No numerical acceptance probability is supported by this review.
